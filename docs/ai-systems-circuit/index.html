The working picture

Trace the whole circuit.

The answer on screen is the endpoint of a route. The model is one component in an arrangement that may include a conversation, retrieved sources, tool calls, external services, and a system that records or reuses results.

01 / GENERATE

The model proposes.

Trained parameters produce candidate text or actions from the input they receive. A proposal can be useful without already being verified or carried out.

02 / CONDITION

The session shapes the answer.

Instructions, history, examples, retrieved context, settings, and available tools change what reaches the model and what it can return. Same model label; different circuit.

03 / ROUTE & FEEDBACK

The surrounding system determines what follows.

A harness selects routes and capabilities, receives results, applies its own state and constraints, and can preserve a sourced result for later work.

The research thread

From fluent answers
to inspectable mechanisms.

Khaotic's AI research follows the relationships that a flat conversation can hide: state, history, typed sources, geometry, constraints, and the consequences of what a system leaves out.

DREAMER / SURVEYOR

Generate, then map.

The dreamer opens possibilities and forms hypotheses. The surveyor checks a candidate against explicit sources, transitions, constraints, and history. The map itself must also be right.

SOURCE / PROJECTION

What does the view leave out?

Two different underlying states can produce the same display. If their next outcomes differ, the visible snapshot is not enough to predict what happens next.

TEXT / RELATION

Similarity is not a dependency.

A nearby passage may mention a definition without being its source. Typed links can connect a term to its definition, proof status, implementation, revision, and observed result.
