I have spent the rest of my life learning across mathematics, physics, biology, computation, game systems and other disciplines. The LEVI experiment brings those histories together.

**I don't want the lab to become an echo chamber.** I need better questions, stronger counterexamples, practitioners with different experience and outside eyes on the test design. This isn't a call for people to accept my theory; it's an invitation to challenge it constructively.

## Research challenge 1 — Test Minimum Five, the actual foundation

**BHUAT proposes one way reality can generate bounded, persistent structure through five independent recursive obligations: ϕ emergence, τ ordering, ψ memory, χ coherence and Λ regulation.** The program already contains an exact local five-state theorem for the registered ordered operator, a Lean-checked fixed-basis support-minimum and an admitted realizer-invariance result.

**What would help:** interrogate the proof scopes, challenge semantic independence, attempt an honest **four-effective-support** body with all hidden/external support counted, design new negative controls, or find a distinct physically discriminating test. A four-dimensional plot or response rank is not by itself the requested counterexample.

**Related:** [Minimum Five](../field-notes/MINIMUM_FIVE.md), [LEVI body thesis](../field-notes/LEVI.md), [Autopoiesis](../field-notes/AUTOPOIESIS.md).

## Research challenge 2 — Thermodynamics and viability

Can the burden/resource analogies be converted into calibrated quantities that predict failure, energy use, maintenance cost or recovery? Is there a compelling conservative/dissipative split, or do conventional models explain the data more economically?

**What would help:** measured power/resource protocols, conservation assumptions, entropy-production estimates, uncertainty budgets or rigorous countermodels.

**Related:** [Complex and hybrid dynamics](../field-notes/COMPLEX_SYSTEMS.md), [Physical science](../field-notes/PHYSICAL_SCIENCE.md).

## Research challenge 3 — Geometry, topology and developmental freedom

The program investigates constrained degrees of freedom, viability geometry, nested holarchies, seams and admissible transitions. Which structures admit useful definitions or proof obligations, and which analogies fail?

**What would help:** dimensional reduction arguments, formal constraints, examples of obstruction, numerical protocols, control-theoretic alternatives and local/global compatibility tests.

**Related:** [Complex dynamics](../field-notes/COMPLEX_SYSTEMS.md), [Holarchy and neurons](../field-notes/HOLARCHY_NEURONS.md).

## Research challenge 4 — Native physical execution

LEVI has recorded native CPU execution and Linux-driver GPU numerical work. Native GPU compute admission and dependable embodied I/O remain active integration tasks.

**What would help:** architecture review, UEFI and GPU diagnostic methods, bounded hardware-interface protocols, trustable observational evidence, source-to-boot provenance and recovery methodology.

**Related:** [LEVI progress](../field-notes/LEVI_PROGRESS.md), [Engineering journal](../field-notes/LEVI_JOURNAL.md).

## Research challenge 5 — Matched tests against current AI and adaptive systems

What experiment could demonstrate that the proposed body-law/history/development mechanisms contribute something measurable beyond a model, memory system, tool controller or recurrent program?

**What would help:** fair baselines, preregistered tasks, ablation design, interruption/migration studies, held-out transfer, resource/energy controls, and independent evaluation.

**Related:** [Benchmark protocol](../field-notes/LEVI_BENCHMARKS.md), [Interactive explorer source](index.html).

## Protected source and responsible review

**The public pages intentionally redact exact BHUAT/5FRE operators, coefficients and proprietary derivations.** A scientific challenge can begin from the public hypotheses, theorem outcomes, proof scopes and reported experiments; a deeper review may require a separately agreed confidential access path. The preserved private research source is not automatically released through an issue or email. [Research disclosure policy](../field-notes/RESEARCH_ACCESS.md).

## How to contribute meaningfully

A concise technical critique is valuable. It can include:
- **The exact claim or equation** being challenged.
- **An alternative explanation**, relevant literature, or a counterexample.
- **A proposed test** with baseline, measurable response and failure criterion.
- **The resources and permissions required**.
- **What success would and would not establish**.

Use **admin@khaotic.ai** for introductions and private or potentially IP-sensitive discussions. A structured GitHub issue template is included in the profile repository for when public Issues are enabled. **Do not publicly post credentials, unpublished code, patents under review, hardware identifiers or personal information.**

This isn't a fixed job offer, a funded fellowship or an institutional collaboration agreement. It is a public research invitation with real work and real questions.
