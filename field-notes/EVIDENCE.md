## How to assess a claim in this program

1. **Origin:** Is it founder intent, an original dated document, or later interpretation?
2. **Source:** Is there a pinned source or schema that actually expresses it?
3. **Execution:** Is a specific experiment, build, boot, body, machine, or user interface observed?
4. **Custody:** Is a version/receipt preserved without resetting the historical record?
5. **Scope:** What boundary was measured, and what does it explicitly *not* prove?
6. **Currency:** Is this a historical result, a source-current implementation, or a live runtime observation?

This ladder protects the work from both overclaiming and losing hard-won historical accomplishments when a different machine, provider or source revision becomes current.
