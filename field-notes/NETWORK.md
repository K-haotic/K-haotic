## More than a laboratory and more than a game

**Khaotic Gaming LLC** is the founder's company; **Khaotic.ai** is a public entry into the wider program; **Recursive Physics Lab** is the research identity. The ecosystem joins first-principles mathematics, engineered compute, modular services, imaginative worlds, civic/geospatial interests and practical businesses.

It is important not to make one layer stand in for another. A public page can be live while a proposed autonomous experience is not. A historical source can be recovered while its matching service is not installed. A **source store** and a **customer storefront** solve different problems.

## KEEL is a store - but be precise about what it stores

**KEEL is the governed source and generation store.** It preserves the source/lineage context needed to know *which implementation, revision and authority* a result came from. Its role includes source custody and settlement. That is different from:

- **GitHub:** a development collaboration, review and public profile platform;
- **Historical Atlas:** a deliberately reduced and reviewed public read model;
- **Adaptive Fabric:** work routing and execution coordination with evidence;
- **Khaotic Commerce:** first-party product, customer and economic design;
- **Whale Fall:** a person-facing experience and potential product surface.

The distinctions matter because conflating a source database, a public site, and a retail checkout would make security, operations and historical claims less accurate.

See the public dossier [KEEL Source and Generation Store](https://khaotic.ai/atlas/research/?dossier=d-keel#research).

## What the world programs mean

**Whale Fall** concerns a broader immersive, navigable, participatory experience. Its current public entrance is real, but the larger design scope should not be mistaken for a finished, self-running universe.

**Machine City** is a long-running source and design program for institutions, roles and coordinated functions. The historical corpus includes preserved records and an independently counted 387/387 recovery of a frozen source family. That is not a census of active autonomous workers.

**Garden / Recursants** captures developmental, learning and rehearsal ideas alongside software and research. A professional role, simulated character, recurring program and authorized AI executor must remain distinct concepts.

**OMMI** predates several of the current integration labels and retains an important founder-origin intention: the own-engine/modular world design should not be reduced to a single third-party renderer or AI service.

**Commerce, media and services** are part of the architecture, not an afterthought. Public storefront and payment availability should be stated only when there is a verified reachable purchase path. Meanwhile [Khaotic.ai](https://khaotic.ai/) and [Support](https://khaotic.ai/support/) are the appropriate public paths for inquiries and support.
