## A life working before the first employment résumé

My father was a **residential contractor**. I worked with him building houses from the time I could hold a hammer until around 17, across the dirt, foundation, structure, roofing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC and finish work. I didn't realize I should be getting paid for this work until I was 15.

That belongs alongside the industrial record. My later roles were repeatedly **lead roles** and culminated in shop-foreman responsibilities, while I continued studying independently and developing Khaotic Gaming.

## Maintaining a complete professional account

The complete source documents contain old contact details and role-specific application material; the public profile uses a curated skill and history synthesis instead of posting those originals.
