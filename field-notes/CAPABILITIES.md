## 10 / What crosses all of it

Some people know how to build the drawing. Some know how to do the labor. Some can troubleshoot the machine. Some can lead the crew or handle the business side.

**I've spent my life moving between all of those positions.**

I can enter an unfamiliar environment, examine the materials and the problem, listen to people who know the system, ask the right questions, learn the missing details, work out a solution and execute it. That adaptability is not a substitute for experience; it is what years of diverse experience have taught me to do.
