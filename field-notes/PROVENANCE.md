## Evidence maturity: what each label means

- **Scoped mathematical result:** a statement with explicit definitions and assumptions; it cannot be transferred to a different operator or physical theory without proof.
- **Executable source:** code exists; that does not prove current deployment or hardware readiness.
- **Recorded simulation / toy result:** the narrow modeled behavior was recorded, not a physical-biological event.
- **Historical physical receipt:** a specific machine, boot, version and test state; does not certify every later version.
- **Draft, bridge, hypothesis, preregistration:** active scientific or engineering questions with defined future tests.

## How to assess a research claim

1. Identify the exact research question, model or declared implementation.
2. Classify the evidence: architecture, code, validation, scoped proof, experimental result or physical receipt.
3. Seek the inputs, controlled comparisons, failure modes and negative controls before accepting a stronger conclusion.
4. Confirm whether an implementation is local, simulated, installed, boot-specific, admitted, externally reproducible or deployed.
5. Request a selective source review or independently reproducible public demonstration where disclosure rights permit.
