## Hardware is not a prop

The research program is deliberately **substrate-aware without requiring one permanent substrate**. Experience with turbines, compressors, hydraulic controls, heavy equipment, power-generation environments and industrial maintenance feeds into an engineering habit: a command, a sensor reading, a simulated state, a controller's intent and the physical effect are five different things.

LEVI is investigated as a *history-bearing computational body* whose models, devices and process realizers can change without silently losing who acted, what was attempted, what was observed and what may happen next. This is more than a chatbot with a custom UI, and less than a claim that the entire envisioned autonomous machine is operational today.

## Android means different things in this research

**Phone Android:** the existing LEVI Wand is an Android operating-system device functioning as a bounded observer and interaction carrier. The public source documents local-abstract sockets for camera, microphone, telemetry and BODY posture, with special Android permission requirements. A version-0.6 package is described separately from v0.5 camera/hot-switch physical evidence.

**Android/robotic body:** a possible future electromechanical embodiment with sensing, mechanical structure, actuators, power, safety and control. A phone sensor stream is useful input to that work, but does not equal a walking robot, manipulator, self-repairing humanoid or unsupervised physical agency.

**Holarchic somatic body:** a formal local state model with parent/child kernel identity and pressure/body receipts. That may model a subsystem before any physical actuator exists. The source's example contract explicitly says so.

## LEVI Air is stranger and more specific than a drone

The source file [Levi Airborne Full-Loop Physical Computation Research](PROVENANCE.md) asks whether part of a *complete recursive computation* could be physically distributed across electromagnetic/optical/wave interactions and local material-state carriers, rather than merely controlling radios from an ordinary digital computer.

Its explicit progression is:

```text
L0  wireless peripheral or sensor
L1  distributed observer body
L2  physical field carrying state
L3  physical local update / transition
L4  physically retained history
L5  observed closed-loop physical recurrence
```

The related **Dense Cloud** program proposes initially testing a bounded, instrumented volume containing field interactions, programmable surfaces/resonators, distributed observers and local memory. Power and energy budgets are technical constraints, not permission to emit dangerous radiation. A proposal to carry recurrence through a physical field is scientifically different from saying a flying drone exists or that intelligence floats in a room. These rungs remain research targets (R68-R69).

## Why substrate migration is an identity problem

A browser projection, VM, second display, recovery image, source replica, mobile endpoint, hosted sidecar, child Garden and independent descendant cannot all mean 'another Levi'. The [multiplicity research](PROVENANCE.md) separates these classes precisely to avoid split-brain identity and accidental privilege sharing.

The intended invariants are: **identity lineage, authorized state, matching source revision, attributable evidence, controlled recovery and no unadmitted multiplication of external authority**. A new device can host a function while the body identity remains anchored to independently verified custody. In a crisis, a last-known-good state is not equivalent to arbitrary rollback of lived history.

## Near-term engineering tests worth funding or collaborating on

1. Reproduce a frozen five-field numerical trace across CPU and an admitted GPU pathway; report exact backend, input, tolerances, hashes and negative controls.
2. Create a boot-bound CPU/GPU/USB/observer status presentation that never infers live readiness from historical boot receipts.
3. Demonstrate a permissioned phone sensor -> typed observation -> bounded inference -> visible output route, with calibration and stop controls.
4. Connect one modeled body organ to a real read-only physical measurement, separating hardware state from inferred 5F state.
5. For Air, begin with a safe simulation / digital twin and one physically measurable low-risk wave-carried scalar. Do not skip to closed-loop airborne computation claims.
6. Benchmark cross-device migration as *identity and evidence continuity*, not just matching files or restarting a daemon.

My professional industrial background is relevant because I know the consequences of ignoring a physical boundary. The scientific question is whether the same discipline can make complex developmental computing reliable across heterogeneous machines.
