## Why the chronology is not just a commit timeline

A commit graph answers **when source changed**. The historical Atlas tries to answer a harder set of questions:

- **Who originally defined the intention?**
- **Which concepts and technologies existed at that time?**
- **Which source generations were actually admitted, recovered, or reconciled?**
- **Which experiments measured a result on a specific machine?**
- **Which claims remain interpretations or future intentions?**
- **Which public statements have earned their evidence boundary?**

The research program retains original OMMI/world designs rather than reducing them to later infrastructure; it distinguishes elapsed time from causal order and an internal recursive tau coordinate; it keeps alternative computational realizations and historical challengers visible rather than rewriting them into one slogan.

The published version of this record is deliberately bounded: [the historical graph](https://khaotic.ai/atlas/), [lineage views](https://khaotic.ai/atlas/#lineages), and [research dossiers](https://khaotic.ai/atlas/research/). These represent documented public projections. They are not an exhaustive export of the private archival estate.

## Legal and scientific chronology remain separate

Historical research source anchors and application filing dates should not be used to infer the complete scope, legal effect or validity of any patent claim. The private patent and source estate is maintained under governed custody. Public language here states only the two recorded U.S. filing events and does not make grant or priority conclusions.
